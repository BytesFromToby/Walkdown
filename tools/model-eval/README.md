The labeled sets that measured the labeling models, moved here on 2026-10-29 when Label was parked
(restructure step 5, decision 5-B). They are not graded in a run. They are the measuring set for the
model work, which waits until the restructure is done.

- `labels-repo/`: look-alike pairs written for the test.
- `real-lines/`: 119 five-line excerpts of lines stage 6 asked about in three audited repositories
  (MIT-licensed; see `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`).
- `answers.json`: their hand labels, in the grader's answer format.
- `real-lines-review.md`: the labeling review sheet.

What they measured: `docs/MODEL-ACCURACY.md`. The files are byte-exact (`.gitattributes`).
